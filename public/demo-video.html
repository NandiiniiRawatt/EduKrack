The Water Cycle

An Educational Explanation by EduKrack AI

Evaporation

Heat from the sun causes water from oceans, lakes, and rivers to turn into water vapor, rising into the atmosphere.

Condensation

As water vapor rises, it cools and turns back into tiny water droplets, forming clouds.

Precipitation

When droplets get heavy enough, they fall as rain, snow, sleet, or hail, returning water to Earth's surface.

Collection & Infiltration

Water collects in oceans, lakes, and rivers. Some seeps into the ground as groundwater, replenishing aquifers.

Runoff & Transpiration

Water flows across land as runoff. Plants absorb groundwater and release it as vapor through transpiration.

The Continuous Cycle

The water cycle continuously repeats, ensuring water circulation throughout Earth's ecosystems.
